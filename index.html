About FlightRadar360

Made for the virtual aviation community.

FlightRadar360 is an independent community project focused on making it easier for GeoFS pilots to see the traffic around them and share useful flight information.

Built to be useful, open, and pilot-controlled.

Explore the public GeoFS multiplayer radar without signing in. The separate Connected Users map is opt-in: the Tampermonkey script only shares your simulator position and the flight details you enter after you enable sharing. You can turn sharing off at any time. FlightRadar360 is not an official GeoFS service.

FlightRadar360 documentation

Know what the radar can—and can’t—show.

FlightRadar360 combines different sources for its two map modes. Review these requirements before troubleshooting a missing aircraft.

GeoFS Live requires multiplayer

To see yourself on the public GeoFS Live radar, GeoFS multiplayer must be enabled and your aircraft must be present in GeoFS’s public multiplayer map feed. FlightRadar360 cannot add a flight that GeoFS does not include in that feed.

Connected Users does not require multiplayer

The Tampermonkey map reads your local GeoFS aircraft position and shares it directly when you opt in. You do not need to join GeoFS multiplayer, but GeoFS must be open, your aircraft must be loaded, and the script must be installed and sharing.

Sharing must be enabled

Connected Users sharing is off by default. Enable it in the FlightRadar360 GeoFS panel and confirm the disclosure. Your position, callsign, route, and optional squawk are visible to visitors of that map while sharing is active.

Connected aircraft can take a moment to appear

The script refreshes shared positions periodically and the map checks for updates while Connected Users is open. An aircraft may take several seconds to appear. Stale records are hidden after 45 seconds without an update.

Flight plan auto-fill has limits

Departure and arrival auto-fill only when GeoFS exposes departure and destination waypoints with four-character ICAO identifiers. Otherwise, enter those airport codes manually. Callsign is required; squawk is optional.

Traffic and data are not guaranteed

  • GeoFS multiplayer data and Connected Users sharing depend on GeoFS, network availability, and their respective services.
  • METAR reports may be cached; always use official sources for operational decisions.
  • FlightRadar360 is an independent community project and is not an official GeoFS service.
